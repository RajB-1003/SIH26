02   THE CHALLENGE

The Challenge

Define the problem clearly before presenting the technology.

PROBLEM STATEMENT
• Limited Screening Access: Frequent health monitoring is difficult where medical equipment and healthcare professionals are not readily available.
• Untapped Smartphone Potential: A smartphone can capture facial, eye, voice, cough, and movement signals, but these capabilities remain largely fragmented.
• No Personal Health Baseline: Existing solutions often measure isolated parameters rather than continuously identifying meaningful changes from an individual's normal health pattern.
TARGET USERS
• Beneficiaries: Rural & low-resource populations, elderly individuals, chronic care patients, and remote workers.
• Stakeholders: Family caregivers, village health workers, doctors, campus clinics, & telemedicine providers.
• Context / Location: Primary healthcare centers, home care, and remote diagnostic screening.
TECHNICAL & SOCIAL IMPACT
• $0 Extra Hardware Cost: Turns any basic smartphone into a multi-modal health monitor without buying expensive medical devices.
• Instant Universal Access: Empowers rural communities & home patients to perform 1-minute non-invasive health checks anytime.
• Early Risk Prevention: Catches hidden warning signs in vitals, fatigue, stress, & breathing before emergencies occur.
PROBLEM IN ONE LINE
“How might we fuse smartphone camera video, eye tracking, speech acoustics, and cough sound waves into a single AI app for instant, zero-cost health screening?”

06   SOLUTION WORKFLOW

How It Works

A simple end-to-end workflow: input → pre-process → intelligence → decision → output.

01 // INPUT
📱 Sensor Capture
• Camera Video: Captures 30 FPS face video & upper-face eye close-up.
• Mic Audio: 5s voice sample + 2 cough sound bursts.
• Zero Hardware: Standard phone camera & mic ($0 cost).
02 // PRE-PROCESS
⚙️ Signal Cleaning
• MediaPipe: Locks facial landmarks & tracks iris boundaries in real time.
• rPPG Filter: POS optical model + Butterworth filter wipes motion noise.
• Audio VAD: Strips room echoes & ambient background noise.
03 // INTELLIGENCE
🧮 Multi-Track AI
• rPPG Engine: Computes Pulse Rate, SpO2 %, HRV & Breaths/min.
• PERCLOS Model: Measures eye closure ratio % & gaze speed.
• Acoustic AI: Pitch trembles & audio CNN cough patterns.
04 // DECISION
⚖️ Clinical Decision
• Biomarker Fusion: Combines vitals, fatigue, stress & cough strain.
• Risk Scoring: Compares results against baseline thresholds.
• Early Alerts: Flags vital drops before emergencies occur.
05 // OUTPUT
📊 Actionable Output
• Vitals Dashboard: Real-time visual display of all 4 scan results.
• Healio Chatbot: Explains technical numbers in plain language.
• Medical Diary: Saves trend log + sends remote guardian alerts.

AI & DIAGNOSTIC MODELS
📈
rPPG & Face Vitals
Google MediaPipe + POS/CHROM Model + FFT: Skin ROI tracking & color channel filtering to extract Heart Rate (BPM), SpO2 %, & HRV.
👁️
Eye Fatigue Model
MediaPipe Iris 468 Mesh + PERCLOS (ISO Standard): Calculates eyelid closure ratio ≥ 80% shut & saccadic gaze velocity for Drowsiness % & Focus %.
🎙️
Speech Acoustic Model
Librosa VAD + Praat Pitch (F0) & Shimmer + OpenSMILE: Extracts vocal micro-trembles, pitch stability, & MFCCs to compute Stress % & Voice Stability.
😮‍💨
Cough Airway AI
STFT 2D Mel-Spectrogram + ResNet-18 Audio CNN: Classifies 1D cough sound waves into Dry vs Wet mucus patterns & Airway Strain Score %.
